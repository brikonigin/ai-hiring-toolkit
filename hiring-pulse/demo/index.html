    Start here

    1. Check Needs action on Overview. These are this week’s follow-ups. Select one to see the people or roles behind it.

    2. Set your Focus. Pick your team, role or name so the numbers cover what you own.

    3. Ask a question in the box at the top. Answers show the rows they used and when that data is from.

    How cards are grouped

    Every card carries a colored tag for the question it answers. Select a tag above the cards to show only cards like it.

    Act now Follow-ups and waiting candidates. On time Targets, projections and time to fill. Health and fairness Impact, sources, drop-off and offers. What changed What moved since the last weekly update.

    Cards labeled Follows focus narrow with the Focus bar. Cards labeled All roles, like Impact and Why candidates leave, always cover everything, so small groups can’t be singled out.

    Reading colors

    Red past target or over a waiting limit. Amber at risk, or data more than a week old. Green on track or improving. Blue the normal state. Colors are always paired with words.

    When the dashboard can’t answer

    Under every answer, pick a person and copy a ready-written message to them. The ask box matches questions to the sheet’s columns rather than reasoning like a chatbot, and it says so when it can’t match instead of guessing.

    How the numbers are defined

    Roles and targets
    Target
    The hire date agreed with the hiring manager, or 45 days after opening if none.
    Status
    Past target: the target has passed. At risk: 14 days or less left and no one in final stages. Offer out: an offer is extended. On track: none of these.
    Projected fill
    Stages left between a role’s furthest candidate and an accepted offer, times the typical days candidates spend in a stage.
    Candidates and stages
    Stages
    Pre-screen, Recruiter screen, Hiring manager interview, Exercise or 3rd round, Executive screen, Offer, Offer accepted. Each role’s own interview loop is mapped onto these.
    Final stages
    Exercise or 3rd round, Executive screen and Offer.
    Waiting
    Flagged after more than 2 days waiting on interview feedback.
    Drop-off
    Share of candidates who reached each step compared with the step before, including those who have since left.
    Speed, offers and sources
    Time to fill
    Days from a role opening to an accepted offer.
    Offer acceptance
    Offers accepted out of offers made. A declined offer is a candidate who withdrew at the Offer stage.
    Source recorded
    Share of candidates in process whose source is anything other than “Not recorded” or “Added manually.”
    Asks answered
    Share of each week’s asks to hiring teams answered within 7 days.
