# Company configuration

Fill this in once per organization. Every skill in this toolkit reads it, so a new company, location or value is a one-file change.

> Everything below is an **example**. Replace it with your organization's approved content before use.

## Organization
- Company name: Acme Co. (example)
- Approver for hiring standards and flagged compliance items: People leader
- Second reviewer: Talent lead
- Applicant tracking system (ATS): your ATS
- Document tool: your team's shared document tool

## Values (ask each once per interview loop, score 1-4)

| Value | Look for | Red flag (the "NOT" line) |
|---|---|---|
| **Ownership** (example) | Sees problems early, takes them on, follows through | Waits to be told; blames others; glorifies overwork |
| **Curiosity** (example) | Asks questions first, learns fast, uses AI and new tools thoughtfully while owning the judgment | Assumes they know; uses tools without judgment |
| **Candor** (example) | Shares a clear view, changes their mind with evidence | Hides views to avoid conflict; contrarian for its own sake |
| **Customer Care** (example) | Anticipates needs, welcomes everyone, owns problems end to end | "Not my job"; avoids hard conversations |

## Defaults (change only with approval)
- Decision rule: a 1 on any competency, or two or more 2s, means no; any values score of 1 means no.
- Screen-to-offer target: about 15 business days.
- Scorecards due within 24 hours; decisions within 2 business days of each round; candidate told within 1 business day of a decision.
- Candidate-facing text never states headcount, growth rate, fundraising or hiring strategy.
