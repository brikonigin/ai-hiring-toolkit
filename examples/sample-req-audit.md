# Requisition Audit — Acme Co. (fictional example)

> **Everything in this document is invented** to show what the [req-audit](../skills/req-audit/SKILL.md) skill produces. Acme Co., its roles, people and numbers are not real. People appear by role only.

*Internal and confidential. Data from ATS export and hiring manager intakes, March 2–6.*

---

## What changed after review meetings

Reviews with the People leader and the Head of Operations on March 6 confirmed most findings and corrected three:

- **Agency candidates are in the ATS.** The draft said they weren't. The real gap is that agency fees and time to fill aren't tracked.
- **The Data Analyst req stays open after its current offer.** Finance approved a second analyst, so it no longer counts as a filled req to close.
- **The recruiter drafts interview plans; hiring managers approve them.** Agreed by both reviewers.

## Executive summary

Acme has a **throughput problem, not a candidate problem**. Of 12 open reqs, 3 are filled but still open, 8 are past their target date, and about 2,400 applications haven't been reviewed. Interview loops are generally sound. The bottlenecks are req hygiene, scheduling ownership, recruiter capacity and two compliance gaps in job posts.

## Ranked action plan

| # | Problem | Evidence | Fix | When |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | People mid-process are stuck | 14 of 21 candidates waiting to be scheduled have waited 10+ days; the longest has waited 41 days. 3 final rounds await a decision, the longest 16 days. | Schedule or release every one. Set a 2-business-day service level for scheduling and for decisions after a final round. | This week |
| 2 | Filled reqs still open | 3 reqs with accepted offers still collect applicants. | Close, unpublish, send a "position filled" note, and tell the agency. | This week |
| 3 | Compliance gaps in posts | AI screening is on for roles in a jurisdiction that requires a bias audit and candidate notice. No post carries EEO or accommodation language. | Pause AI screening there until a policy is approved and counsel has reviewed it. Add the standard footer to every post. | This week |
| 4 | Plan and reqs don't match | 3 approved positions have no req; 2 open reqs have no approved position. | Reconcile with Finance. No req opens without an approved position. | Next 2–4 weeks |
| 5 | No one owns scheduling | One recruiter on 12 reqs, no coordinator. Root cause of #1. | Name a scheduling owner per req; send availability requests from the ATS the day a candidate advances. | Next 2 weeks |
| 6 | Applicant backlog | About 2,400 unreviewed. Customer Support Specialist: 780 untouched after 45 days. | After #2, clear the backlog; add knockout questions and a 5-business-day review service level. | Next 2 weeks |
| 7 | Interview quality unverified | 81% of interview rounds have one scorecard. No req uses a job template. | Two trained interviewers on each decision round, with a rubric and scorecard per round. | This month |
| 8 | No trustworthy reporting | Target dates are stale; 4 of 9 recent hires have no recorded source. | Make source a required field, reset dates, set baselines. | This month |

## Decisions and open questions

| Decision | Agreed with | Detail |
|---|---|---|
| Recruiter owns interview plans | People leader, Head of Ops | Recruiter drafts from templates; hiring managers approve. |
| Recruiting ends at contract signing | Head of Ops | People Ops owns onboarding after that. |
| Customer Support is an always-open req | Head of Ops | One evergreen req instead of one per location. |

**Open:** Who approves offers when the People leader is out? Is the agency's fee structure still right now that a recruiter is in seat? Which time-to-fill target applies: 40 or 45 days?

## Baseline scorecard

| Measure | Target | Today | Gap and what closes it |
|---|---|---|---|
| Offer acceptance | 90% | 71% (10 of 14) | Ask about pay and competing offers at the recruiter screen; approve offers within 48 hours. |
| On-time hiring | 90% accepted within 2 weeks of target | 8 of 12 reqs past target | Reset every target date at intake. |
| Time to fill | Under 45 days (to confirm) | About 52 days median | Stage service levels: review 5 business days, scheduling 2, decision 2, offer approval 2. |
| Responsiveness | 1 business day | 21 waiting to be scheduled; ~2,400 never contacted | Clear the backlog, then track misses weekly. |
| ATS data quality | Complete and current | Wrong hiring manager on 4 reqs; source missing on 4 of 9 hires | Required fields and a weekly check. |
| Candidate experience | Measured | Not measured | Short survey after the final round. |

## Best practice vs. current state (excerpt)

| Area | Best practice | What we have | Reqs affected |
|---|---|---|---|
| Applicant review | Every applicant actioned within a set service level | ~2,400 unreviewed | 12 |
| Req lifecycle | Close the req the day an offer is accepted, unless it's a repeat hire | 3 filled reqs still open | 3 |
| Headcount link | Every req tied to an approved position | 2 not linked | 2 |
| Job templates | Standard stages, scorecards and criteria | None used | 12 |
| Screening criteria | Mirror the post's must-haves only | 3 reqs screen on criteria the post doesn't list | 3 |
| Pay language | Clear range, nothing that discourages negotiation | Posts call the range "not for negotiation" | 9 |

## Compliance exposure

- **AI in hiring:** AI screening is active where a bias audit and candidate notice are required. Paused pending a policy and counsel review (see `shared/compliance-process.md`).
- **EEO and accommodation:** missing from every post. Standard footer added (see `shared/compliance-posting.md`).
- **Pay transparency:** ranges are posted, but the disclaimer reads as contradictory. Rewording proposed.
