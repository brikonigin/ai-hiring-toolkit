## Round 2: Hiring Manager (sample questions)

**1. Walk me through an account you noticed was at risk before the customer said anything. What did you see, and what did you do?**
Look for: a specific signal, a named action, and the outcome. Red flag: only reacted after the customer escalated.

**2. Value (Candor): Tell me about a time you disagreed with your manager about a customer decision.**
Look for: stated a clear view with evidence and stayed open to changing it. Red flag: stayed silent to avoid conflict, or argued without listening.

**Advance if:** no 1s and no more than one 2.

| Competency | 4 | 3 | 2 | 1 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Account health | Spots risk early from data and conversations; acts with a plan and owner | Spots risk with some prompting; acts | Notices risk late; vague actions | No evidence of monitoring health |

## Exercise prompt (candidate-facing excerpt)
> You'll lead a 30-minute renewal conversation with a fictional customer whose usage dropped last quarter. You'll get the same background packet as every candidate, and we expect about 2-3 hours of prep over 5 business days. Please give us your work, not an AI's work. If you'd like an accommodation, such as extra time or a different format, just let your recruiter know.
