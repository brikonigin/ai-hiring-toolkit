# Interview standards: loop design

If a source conflicts with a standard, flag the conflict for the hiring manager and People leader rather than choosing.

## Loop shape
- **Default loop:** Recruiter Screen (30 min) → Hiring Manager (45-60 min) → Skills Exercise or Panel (60 min, mid-loop) → Executive Close (30 min) → References → Debrief. About 3 hours of interviews.
- **Fewer rounds, not more.** If two rounds test the same competency, cut one.
- **Exercise early.** Send the prompt the same day a candidate passes the screen.
- **No separate values interview.** Each value is asked exactly once across the first three rounds, labeled `Value (<name>): …`, and scored 1-4 in that round.

## How every round runs
1. Same questions, same order, for every candidate. Probe deeper; don't swap or add topics.
2. Score every competency 1-4 in the ATS within 24 hours, with one line of evidence per score, before reading anyone else's feedback.
3. Apply the decision rule from `company-config.md`. The debrief discusses only disagreements.
4. Schedule the next round within 3 business days of a pass. Decide within 2 business days. Tell the candidate within 1 business day of a decision.
5. Remind interviewers at 24 hours; escalate to the hiring manager at 48 hours.
6. Share the loop, interviewer names and prep time with every candidate after the screen.
7. Offer accommodations in every invite and exercise prompt.
8. Only trained interviewers score.

## Exercises
- Fictional scenario, never real company work. Same prompt, packet, prep time and scripted objections for every candidate.
- About 2-3 hours of prep at most, with 5 business days to prepare. Say so in the prompt.
- Include: "Please give us your work, not an AI's work," and how to request accommodations.
