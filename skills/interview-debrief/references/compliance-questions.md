# Compliance: what interviewers and applications may never ask

Last reviewed October 2026. Not legal advice. Approved and reviewed quarterly by the approver in `company-config.md`. Apply the rules for every location in scope (role, candidate and remote work location); if unknown, apply the strictest rule and mark `[Location to confirm]`.

| Topic | Never ask | Ask instead |
|---|---|---|
| Age | Age, birth date, graduation years, "how long until you retire" | Nothing; prefer competencies to years of experience |
| Citizenship and national origin | Citizenship, birthplace, immigration status, native language, accent, ancestry | "Are you legally authorized to work in the US?" and "Will you now or in the future require sponsorship?" |
| Disability and health | Medical conditions, disabilities, medications, sick days, workers' comp, genetic or family medical history | "Can you perform the essential functions of this role, with or without reasonable accommodation?" (functions written down first) |
| Sex, family and caregiving | Pregnancy, plans for children, childcare, marital or caregiver status, spouse's job | Schedule or travel requirements, asked of everyone the same way |
| Sexual orientation and gender identity | Any question | Nothing |
| Religion | Religion, holidays observed, community membership | Schedule requirements, asked of everyone the same way |
| Race, color and appearance | Race, color, hair texture or protective hairstyles (CROWN Acts in NY, CA, IL and others), height or weight (NYC) | Nothing |
| Criminal history | Arrests, convictions, background checks before a conditional offer (NYC Fair Chance Act; similar rules in CA, IL and many cities) | Nothing until after a conditional offer, then the fair-chance process |
| Pay history | Current or past salary, bonus or equity (banned in NY, CA, IL and many other places) | "What are your pay expectations?" after sharing the posted range |
| Credit and unemployment | Credit history (NYC), current unemployment (NYC) | Nothing |
| Military | Type of discharge | Job-related skills gained in service |
| Other | Union membership, political activity, social media passwords, "culture fit" | Job-related, values-based behavioral questions |
