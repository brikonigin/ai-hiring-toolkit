# Write a job description

Write a job description a candidate can read in three minutes and see themselves in: specific, warm, honest about the role, and compliant in every location it's posted. Read `references/company-config.md` first.

## Steps
1. **Gather** only what's missing: title, job family, IC or manager track, level, reporting line, locations and work setup, team specifics. If given only a title, state assumptions and generate.
2. **Pay.** Use the organization's approved pay bands for each posting location. Never paste internal band tables into candidate text; show only the final range and mark it `[Confirm with Finance]`.
3. **Write** the sections below, in order.
4. **Final check** with `references/compliance-posting.md` and `references/compliance-questions.md`; list open items as `[Owner to confirm: …]`.

## Sections
1. **Company summary:** the organization's approved boilerplate.
2. **What your experience will be:** 2-3 paragraphs of prose on the day-to-day, collaborators, the manager, business impact, tools, and the real location and office expectations.
3. **Why you belong here and how you'll grow:** each value shown as real behavior for this role.
4. **What success looks like:** 5-7 "You will…" bullets.
5. **What you'll bring:** 6-8 bullets, Required and Preferred marked. Skills and evidence, not years or degrees unless legally required. Include AI fluency (active daily use, not awareness).
6. **How work affects your life:** the real working setup, benefits and mission.
7. **Compensation:** level, track, base range per posting location, OTE and variable split for commission roles, "Equity: included in offer package" (never amounts).
8. **Application questions:** 2-4, tied to must-haves, each with "How to evaluate" guidance. Never salary history, age, graduation dates or anything off-limits.
9. **Standard closing sections:** how the company uses AI in hiring, how candidates may use AI in their application, and equal opportunity with an accommodations line. Use approved text; if missing, write `[Paste approved section]` rather than inventing policy. Where AI screens NYC candidates, include the required notice and alternative-process option.

## Voice
Warm, human, specific, second person. No "rockstar," "ninja," "work hard, play hard," "hustle" or "fast-paced family" language. Never state headcount, growth rate, fundraising or hiring strategy.

## Final check
- [ ] Pay range for every posting location, including remote.
- [ ] No years or degree gates without a legal reason; no age signals; no "culture fit."
- [ ] AI fluency included; each value shown as behavior.
- [ ] Application questions have evaluation guidance and nothing off-limits.
- [ ] Closing sections present, with accommodations.
- [ ] No internal notes, band tables or company growth details in candidate-facing text.
