# Build a talent map

Give the recruiter and hiring manager a shared, sourced picture of where this role's talent is and what it costs, before outreach starts. Read `references/company-config.md` first.

## Rules
- **No real people.** Describe an ideal persona ("this person," "they"), never named candidates or prospects. No scoring or ranking of people.
- **Persona language is EEO-safe:** no personal traits or proxies for them; credentials restated as evidence of skill.
- **Free, public sources only.** No paid people-search tools.
- **Every number has a source and date.** If a value isn't found, write "Not stated" or "Not checked." Never estimate silently.
- **Never in a map:** the company's headcount, growth rate, hiring strategy, requisition numbers or candidate names. Internal pay bands stay internal.
- **Confidence on every finding:** High (official data or 5+ data points), Medium (3-4, a proxy, or one search), Low (under 3, or not calibrated with the hiring manager).

## Modes
- **Quick:** a role the company may hire for. One research pass.
- **Full:** an open role. Separate passes for intake notes, live postings, and market and movement data.

## Steps
1. Ask once: role and mode, level (or a 3-level range if unknown), whether the hiring manager has calibrated the must-haves.
2. Reuse shared research checked in the last 30 days for this role family; research only the gaps.
3. Build four tables:
   - **Target companies:** Company | Tier (1 Direct, 1 Domain, 2 Skills, 3 Movement) | Why their people fit | Titles | Caution | Movement signal (dated) | Source.
   - **Competing demand:** open postings with company, title, location, work model, posted pay and pay basis, whether the level matches, posted date, link.
   - **Market:** pay position against internal bands, supply signals, movement trends.
   - **Sources:** every source with date checked.
4. Write the persona, 3-5 findings with confidence, and **5 dated next steps** with owners.
5. Update the existing map for a role in place; never duplicate it.

## Impact
Tag outreach from a map with a candidate source like "Talent map outreach" in the ATS. Report, in aggregate only: candidates sourced from maps, offers accepted, and average days open for mapped versus unmapped roles. Never per-person data.

## Refresh
Monthly: refresh shared research, refresh active maps, flag stale facts, report impact. The refresh never sends messages.
