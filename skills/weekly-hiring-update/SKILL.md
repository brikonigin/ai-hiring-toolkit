# Weekly hiring update

Prepare one clear weekly update per hiring channel so every hiring manager knows where their role stands, what's needed from them, and by when. Read `references/company-config.md` first.

## Principles
- **Drafts only.** Every message is saved as a draft for the recruiter to review and send. The skill never sends messages and never contacts candidates; anything for a candidate is a suggestion to the recruiter.
- **Accuracy beats completeness.** Never invent or carry forward a number, stage, date or ask that wasn't verified this run. When in doubt, leave it out.
- **The ATS is the source of truth** for roles, hiring managers, stages and sources.
- **Fresh data or no numbers.** If the pipeline data wasn't refreshed today, omit counts for that role and tell the recruiter why.
- **Verify before asking.** An ask is only included if the decision is that person's, it's unanswered, and it's needed to move the role. Anything already resolved (a reply, an ATS move, a booked calendar event) is not re-asked.
- **Neutral, blame-free language.** Never "stuck," "late," "slow" or "overdue" in a shared channel. Use neutral counts: "2 waiting 7+ days on a next step."
- **Privacy.** In broad channels, show candidate counts, not names. Candidate names appear only where the audience already has access, and always link to the ATS record.

## Steps
1. **Holiday check:** on a company holiday, draft nothing and ask the recruiter whether to run another day.
2. **Read sources** for the window since the last update: the pipeline export (roles, stages, days open, applicants, flags), each hiring channel and its threads, the recruiter's direct messages, ATS notification emails, and the calendar (interviews, debriefs, out-of-office).
3. **Choose reportable roles:** active, with an ATS link and fresh data. Closed, filled, unclear or channel-less roles go to the recruiter's to-do list instead.
4. **Route waiting candidates:** waiting on feedback becomes one ask to that hiring manager covering all their candidates; scheduling owned by recruiting never becomes an ask.
5. **Draft per channel:** role heading linked to the ATS, a one-line numbers summary with status against the target date, highlights, asks (person · what's needed · what it unblocks · by when), and next steps.
6. **Report to the recruiter** (under ~450 words): what was drafted, data freshness, every waiting candidate with a suggested message, unverified items, and their own to-dos.
7. **Log the run** and update the pipeline history so week-over-week trends can be measured.

## Measures tracked over time
Days open versus target, candidates waiting past the limit, application review backlog, and time from screen to offer.
