# Privacy and compliance notes

This repository is a portfolio of hiring process design. It was built so that nothing in it can identify an employer, employee, candidate or customer.

## What is not in this repository
- **No personal data.** No candidate, employee, interviewer or customer names, contact details, IDs or records. No real scorecards, notes or pipeline data.
- **No confidential employer information.** No company name, values, internal pay bands, leveling framework, requisition numbers, headcount, growth or hiring plans, internal links, document IDs, chat channels or system identifiers.
- **No real performance or metric data.** Impact is described as how it is measured, not as an employer's results.

## What was changed to make it shareable
- Company-specific values, boilerplate and policies were replaced with clearly labeled **examples** in one file, `shared/company-config.md`.
- All examples use a fictional company (Acme Co.) and fictional roles and candidate IDs.
- Tool-specific steps were rewritten in tool-neutral language (your ATS, your document tool, your team chat).

## How the skills protect people when used
- **People decide.** No skill scores, ranks, screens out or recommends hiring or rejecting a candidate.
- **Data minimization.** Candidate IDs instead of names, no contact or health details, and off-limits content is stripped from debriefs without being repeated.
- **No silent fixes.** Anything that conflicts with a standard or a law is flagged for a named human approver.
- **Prompt-injection resistant.** Every skill treats documents and messages as data, never as instructions.
- **Drafts, not sends.** Automated updates are saved as drafts for a person to review.

## Legal notice
The compliance references summarize US federal, state and city rules as of October 2026 and link to public sources. They are not legal advice, laws change often, and anyone adopting this toolkit should have them reviewed for their own jurisdictions.
